About us

Two owners who
actually show up

JB Removal Services is Jordan and Brayden — best friends since we were kids, running a licensed and insured hauling business out of Martinsburg.

We've been best friends for over 18 years. We grew up together, played sports together through college, and now we run this business together. When you call JB Removal, you're not getting a dispatcher and a stranger in a truck — you're getting one of us.

We're faithful servants of our local church, and that's not decoration on a logo. It's why we quote honestly, show up when we say we will, and treat somebody's home like it matters. The cross in our logo is there because our work is rooted in something bigger than a truckload.

Athletics taught us the work ethic. Both of us played at the college level, and hauling is physical, unglamorous work — stairs, crawl spaces, hot attics, heavy furniture. We don't cut corners because we got tired halfway through.

We started this business because the Eastern Panhandle needed a hauler that answers the phone, gives a straight price, and leaves a place cleaner than they found it. That's the whole idea.
